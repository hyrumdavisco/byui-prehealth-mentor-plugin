GRADUATION PLAN:
Begin with the end in mind
Discuss the year they hope to enter medical school
Plan MCAT April of year before first semester of med school
Take MCAT required classes quickly and together so that less information is lost
Get started on classes that are prerequisites for upper level classes early
Semester structure: 14-15 credits, two hard, one medium, one easy, one religion
Schools observe ability to balance. 1st semester medical school is equivalent to about 23-25 credits.
Take labs
Minors in nonmedical areas of interest are beneficial. Chemistry is great for students who will take a gap year and pursue chemical research.
Start obtaining leadership, service, research, shadowing hours and experiences ASAP (medical schools like to see a timeline w/ underlined extracurriculars)
Take electives according to dream and target med school “additional courses” list
We can recommend professors to students when Brother Felts can't. Give factual and emotionally unbiased recommendations about teachers if they ask.
MCAT COURSES:
Phys 105, 106
Chem 105, 106, 351, 352, 481 (or bio dep. alternative)
Bio 180, 181, 461 (recommended)
General Psychology
Sociology
EXTRACURRICULARS:
Not all experiences are created equal. Experiences are everything.
Leadership (100-200 hours)
Three different experiences
Have experiences lifting others. Imagine the magnitude of a story about turning the life of a person around through consistent efforts and confidence in them.
Understand consequences of decision making
Create and execute plans
Volunteer (300-400 hours):
2-3 different experiences/organizations
Can be medically related but it is not at all preferred
Find underserved populations (small children, special needs, homeless/impoverished)
Stay with a program for a long time, develop relationships, know people’s names and stories
Church/mission experiences should NOT be the main or sole service accounted for. It will be especially useful with compelling stories but should not be the cornerstone
Research (100-200 hours):
Biology research is great but is likely going to be similar to hundreds of other applicants. Does not have to be biology or chemistry related.
Publications matter!
Internships or flex semester programs are great.
The real reason for a research requirement is for a student to demonstrate critical thinking and analysis skills. It’s more about what the student learns in the process than what they discovered in the end.
Shadowing (100-200 hours):
Shadow different physicians in varying disciplines
Hours and experiences can be accumulated quickly
Be professional with physicians. Shadowing is a privilege not a right. Dress well to show respect. “You can always take a tie off”
Asking the doctor questions is enriching for them. Ask at appropriate times.
Be punctual, curious, solicitous to the physician and those who work with them to serve them.
Avoid being chatty with the patient.
GOLDEN QUESTIONS FOR EXTRACURRICULARS:
How will / did this experience demonstrate my pursuit of doctorship? 
How will this better equip me to deal with the challenges of doctorship? 
What did this teach me about putting others before myself? 
How did this inspire me to change the lives of patients?
COMMON MISTAKES:
Starting chemistries too late: at least 4 consecutive semesters of chemistry before MCAT
Taking MCAT too late: June 20th is the absolute latest to take the MCAT if attending school the next year is the goal
Completing generals first: leaves you with a number of hard classes at the end
Starting extracurriculars in your sophomore/junior year: starting early allows you to better balance extracurricular hours with hours for studying and work
Spacing classes out too much: makes for much of your MCAT study time trying to remember information from years ago
Afraid to do school during flex semesters: year round school (or at least a couple of credits in a flex semester) is a great way to complete sequential classes in a short amount of time. Use judgment to determine if not having a break is emotionally challenging.
