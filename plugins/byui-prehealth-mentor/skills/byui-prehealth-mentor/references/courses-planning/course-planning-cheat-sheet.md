The goal is to provide a student balance in their schedule so that they can accommodate extracurricular hours and other activities (including jobs). Crafting an effective semester for Biomedical Science majors revolves around four key steps:
Two hard classes per semester; labs can be included with the lecture as one “class”, for approximately 7-8 credits
One medium hard class a semester; usually an additional BIO or HS course of significance, for 3 credits
One easy class per semester; something very manageable including general education or general electives of the student’s interests, for 1-3 credits
One religion course for 2 credits; after all religion credits have been accounted for (14 credits total), utilize combinations of medium hard and easy courses with remaining hard classes
Of particular importance for Biomedical Science majors is to construct a plan providing 14-16 credits per semester. More credits could be taken but would significantly interfere with their ability to participate in extracurricular hours or employment options.
