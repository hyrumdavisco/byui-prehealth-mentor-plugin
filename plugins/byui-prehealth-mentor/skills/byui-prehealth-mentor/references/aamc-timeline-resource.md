## Explain the overall process

Use the resource to help students connect undergraduate preparation to an application cycle:

1. Learn the admissions process, meet an advisor, build faculty relationships, and explore meaningful experiences.
2. Plan course sequencing, exam readiness, letters, and whether to apply before graduation or after one or more gap years.
3. Prepare and submit applications, complete secondaries, and prepare for interviews while continuing meaningful activities.
4. During a gap year, pursue experiences that fit the student's goals and remain in contact with advising.
5. After acceptance, complete the required decisions, financial-aid steps, and arrangements for matriculation.

The PDF supports individualized planning; it does not establish one mandatory MCAT date or require a gap year.
