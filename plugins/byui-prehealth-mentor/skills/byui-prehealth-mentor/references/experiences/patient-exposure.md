Patient Exposure
Resource status — collected guidance, not a requirement list. Experience types, local openings, certifications, licensing, and employer policies change; verify directly with the current employer, program, and official state or school source before acting.


Pre-med students benefit from obtaining clinical hours before entering medical school for several reasons. Firstly, gaining practical experience in healthcare settings allows them to apply theoretical knowledge learned in the classroom to real-life situations. This hands-on experience helps develop essential skills such as patient interaction, communication, and medical decision-making that are crucial for success in a medical career.
Furthermore, spending time working directly with patients and healthcare professionals provides pre-med students with valuable insights into the realities of working in medicine. They can observe different specialties, understand interdisciplinary collaboration within healthcare teams, and witness firsthand the challenges and rewards of caring for patients.
Moreover, accumulating clinical hours demonstrates a commitment to pursuing a career in medicine beyond academic achievements alone. Admissions committees at medical schools often look favorably upon applicants who have dedicated time to gain practical exposure to the field they aspire to enter.
Overall, engaging in clinical experiences not only enriches pre-medical education but also enhances future physicians’ readiness for the rigors of medical training by fostering empathy toward patients while honing critical skills necessary for providing quality care.
Interacting well with patients is one of the key indicators of a successful future provider. Your experience can be either paid or unpaid. It may or may not require certification. Examples include ER or hospice volunteer, EMT, CNA, MA, phlebotomist, medical scribe, etc.
Competitive applicants will complete 1-2 different experiences each lasting at least 6 months to a year for a total of 100+ hrs.

Medical Assistant
Medical Assistant at Madison Memorial Healthcare in Rexburg, Idaho
Patient Interaction
* Welcome and Check-In: Greet patients warmly, check them in, and ensure a smooth and welcoming experience.
* Medical Histories and Vital Signs: Collect and document detailed medical histories, and measure vital signs including blood pressure, pulse, and temperature.
Clinical Duties
* Assist with Examinations: Support healthcare providers during patient examinations and medical procedures.
* Medication Administration and Testing: Administer medications, perform routine blood draws, and take vital signs.
* Patient Preparation: Prepare patients for outpatient appointments, diagnostic tests, and follow-up procedures.
Administrative Tasks
Note: Administrative tasks are less emphasized at Madison Memorial Healthcare.
* Appointment Scheduling: Facilitate patient appointment scheduling and manage patient records.
* Billing and Insurance: Assist with billing processes, insurance claims, and patient documentation.
* Communication: Answer phones, manage correspondence, and collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
Medical Equipment Maintenance
* Instrument Sterilization: Sterilize and maintain medical instruments and equipment to ensure cleanliness and functionality.
* Supply Management: Monitor medical supplies, manage inventory, and place orders as necessary.
Patient Education
* Medication and Treatment Information: Educate patients on medication instructions, treatment plans, and follow-up care.
* Health Education: Provide basic health education and address patient questions with clear and supportive explanations.
Skills and Qualities
* Effective Communication: Demonstrate compassionate and clear communication with patients and medical staff.
* Attention to Detail: Exhibit meticulous attention to detail in record-keeping and medical tasks.
* Organizational Skills: Manage multiple tasks efficiently and maintain an organized and clean work environment.
* Technical Proficiency: Display competence with medical tools, healthcare software, and basic medical procedures.
Certification
* Certification Requirements: While Idaho does not require medical assistants to be licensed or certified, Madison Memorial Healthcare provides comprehensive training for all new hires.

Care Giver/CNA
Caregiver/CNA at Homestead Assisted Living in Rexburg Idaho 
Role: A caregiver or Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) at Homestead Assisted Living provides essential support and personal care to residents in a senior living community. Their role is crucial in ensuring the comfort, safety, and well-being of residents who may have various levels of physical and cognitive needs.
Responsibilities:
1. Personal Care:
   * Assist residents with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, grooming, and toileting.
   * Help residents with mobility, including transferring in and out of beds, wheelchairs, and assisting with walking.
2. Health Monitoring:
   * Observe and report changes in residents' health or behavior to nursing staff or supervisors.
   * Take and record vital signs, such as blood pressure, temperature, and pulse.
   * Assist with medication reminders and administration under the supervision of a nurse.
3. Emotional Support:
   * Provide companionship and emotional support to residents.
   * Engage in meaningful conversations and activities to enhance residents’ quality of life.
4. Housekeeping and Safety:
   * Ensure residents’ living areas are clean, safe, and organized.
   * Assist with light housekeeping tasks like making beds, tidying up rooms, and laundry.
5. Meal Assistance:
   * Help residents with meal preparation and feeding if needed.
   * Ensure residents receive proper nutrition and adhere to any dietary restrictions.
6. Activity Participation:
      * Support and encourage residents to participate in a wide range of fun, social, and engaging activities. Our residents enjoy exciting options such as horseback riding, hunting, fishing, laser tag, movies, skydiving, and many other adventures.
      * Facilitate or assist with planned activities and outings to maintain engagement and social interaction.
7. Documentation:
   * Maintain accurate and up-to-date records of residents' care and any changes in their condition.
   * Document incidents, care provided, and any observations relevant to residents’ care plans.
8. Communication:
   * Communicate effectively with residents, families, and healthcare professionals.
   * Report any concerns or issues to the appropriate staff members.
Key Qualities:
* Compassionate and patient demeanor
* Good communication skills
* Basic understanding of medical and personal care practices
* Ability to work as part of a team and follow care plans
Additional Benefit:
* CNA License Sponsorship: Homestead Assisted Living will pay for your CNA license if you commit to working with us for six months. This is an excellent opportunity for those looking to start a career in healthcare while gaining valuable experience and certification.
