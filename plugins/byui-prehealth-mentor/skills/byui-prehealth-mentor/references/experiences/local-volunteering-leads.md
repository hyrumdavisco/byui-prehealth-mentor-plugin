PRE-MED VOLUNTEERING OPPORTUNITIES -
EIRMC -
Roles: Front desk, surgical wait room, shuttle drivers, ER volunteers, NICU volunteers
Steps: Go to eirmc.com, click on About us, click on Volunteers. Once you have filled all
of that out, the head of volunteering will reach out
Requirements: TB test within the last year, background check, onboarding, must commit
to 100 hours

Roles: Both clinical and non-clinical opportunities are available
Steps: Email volunteerservices@madisonhealth.org or call 208-359-6559, ext. 7703 to see
